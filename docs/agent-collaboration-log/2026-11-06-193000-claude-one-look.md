# One look across Today, Journal and Practice

6 November 2026, Claude. Owner direction: UI first, back end later. From the walkthrough request that the three screens feel consistent. The owner approved the before and after, keeping the Journal picture.

## What changed

- `GuestPageTop`: one heading and one 2:1 scene, used at the top of all three tabs.
- Today has a "Today" title like the other tabs.
- Journal has "Your words so far" and its desk scene as the first list row. It scrolls away and hides while searching, using the calendar or viewing a day. Its side margins are 24 points, matching Today and Practice.
- Practice has "What do you want to say?", a plain primary button instead of the picture card with a blue strip, and the same search bar as Journal, shown only when there is something to find.

## Trade-off

Journal shows about one and a half entries at first instead of three, until the picture scrolls away.

## Not done

Card corner radii still vary by screen (18 to 22 points), and pushed screens were not reviewed.
