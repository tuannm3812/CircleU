# One look for the inner screens

8 November 2026, Claude. Next step of the owner-approved plan.

## What changed

- Back on Practice, Edit and Reflection is now the same arrow as the system back button. VoiceOver still says "Back".
- An open entry shows its context and feeling chips, shared with the Journal row through `GuestEntryTags`, and they can be changed there.
- The Practice "Find your words" screen drops its scene, so the whole suggestion is visible instead of being cut off by the buttons.
- Content cards on Journal and Practice screens use a 20-point radius.
- The four tabs no longer show their name in the centre of the top bar, at the owner's request (the tab bar already names them). The title stays for VoiceOver. This undoes the "Today" title added in the first one-look pass.

## Not done

Settings needed no change. Small controls keep their own radii.
