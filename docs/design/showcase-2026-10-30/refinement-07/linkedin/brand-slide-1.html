Now · November 2026

Soft two-colour face, open eyes, the scarf's blue. Same character, drawn once and used everywhere.
U

The idea in the mark. The round head is the Circle: the people around you. The white of the face is a U: you, at the centre, and university. No hidden-letter trick forced on it; the penguin came first and the letter was already there.

What stayed: the name, the blue, the three tabs (Today, Journal, Practice) and the promise that nothing leaves the phone. What changed: one drawn character instead of two, a warmer palette, and far less text per screen.
