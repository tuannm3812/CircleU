# LinkedIn post draft

Sharing the next chapter of CircleU, an iPhone project for the conversations that stay with us.

The experience centres on three spaces:

- Today — capture what happened while it’s on your mind
- Journal — revisit your words and reflect at your own pace
- Practice — prepare what you want to say next

This design refinement brings together our Pingu companion, calmer layouts and concise content. The aim is to make a difficult conversation feel easier to approach, one step at a time.

The app is still being refined. I’d love feedback on the visual direction and how clearly the three spaces communicate their purpose.
